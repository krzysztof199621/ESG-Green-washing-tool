general intro 

The BREEAM New Construction V7.1 Resources Hub provides guidance on the updates introduced since Version 7 and supports assessors and project teams in applying the revised requirements. Resources include Technical Standard Summaries, the V7.1 Release Review, FAQs and training modules, helping users understand the key changes and their implications for project assessments. 

BREEAM professionals can access the BREEAM New Construction V7.1 technical manuals and summary of changes via their BREEAM Projects login. All other project teams can access the full technical manuals here . 

Download the BREEAM New Construction Version 7 technical manual (int) 

BREEAM International New Construction Version 7.1 Technical Standard Summary 

Comprehensive Standard for assessing and certifying the sustainability of newly built commercial or residential assets. International New Construction applies in all countries except where a local adaptation has been created. 
Download 

Download the BREEAM New Construction Version 7 technical manual 

BREEAM UK New Construction: Commercial Version 7.1 Technical Standard Summary 

Comprehensive standard for assessing and certifying the sustainability of newly built assets in the UK. UK New Construction is tailored specifically for commercial projects in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. International New Construction will apply in all other countries.

A comprehensive overview highlighting the key developments in BREEAM New Construction Version 7.1, including methodological changes and major refinements from Version 6.
